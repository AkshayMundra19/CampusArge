University of Pennsylvania

Location: Philadelphia, United States

Penn emphasizes interdisciplinary and practical education, home to the nation's first business school (Wharton) and a strong tradition in medicine, law, and the social sciences.

Research Focus

Business and finance (Wharton), medicine, bioengineering, nursing science, communication (Annenberg), and social policy.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Wharton School (Business), Engineering and Applied Science, Nursing, Medicine (Perelman), Law, Dental Medicine, Veterinary Medicine, Education, Design (Weitzman), Social Policy and Practice, Annenberg (Communication)
