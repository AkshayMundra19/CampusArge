Whitman College

Location: Walla Walla, United States

Whitman is a selective liberal arts college in the Pacific Northwest known for its rigorous academics and strong outdoor and environmental programs.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Liberal Arts College
  • Accreditation: NWCCU Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $78,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,500 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 50 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 117 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research in environmental studies and the natural sciences.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences), Environmental Studies
