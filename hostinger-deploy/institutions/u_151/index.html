Westmont College

Location: Santa Barbara, United States

Westmont is an evangelical Christian liberal arts college emphasizing the integration of Christian faith with rigorous academics.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: WSCUC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $66,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 12:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,200 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: N/A
  • Campus Acreage: 111 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research in the natural sciences and Christian studies scholarship.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural and Behavioral Sciences, Social Sciences), Economics and Business, Kinesiology
