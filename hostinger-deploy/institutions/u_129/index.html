Saint Mary's College of California

Location: Moraga, United States

Saint Mary's College is a Lasallian Catholic liberal arts college known for its Collegiate Seminar great-books program and integration of liberal arts with professional schools.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: WSCUC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $68,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 10:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,600 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 700 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 420 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research in the sciences, and business ethics scholarship.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts and Sciences, Economics and Business, Education, Kalmanovitz School of Education, Collegiate Seminar Program
