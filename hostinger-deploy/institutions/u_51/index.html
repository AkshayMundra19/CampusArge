Washington and Lee University

Location: Lexington, United States

Washington and Lee is a small liberal arts university with an honor system dating back generations, offering an undergraduate college and a school of law.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: SACSCOC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $76,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 8:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,822 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 396 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 430 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research in the sciences and humanities, and legal scholarship at the School of Law.

Academic Programs

College (Arts and Sciences), Williams School of Commerce Economics and Politics, School of Law
