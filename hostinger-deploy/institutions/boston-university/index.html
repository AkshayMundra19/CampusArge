Boston University

Location: Boston, United States

BU is a large, urban research university offering a broad range of undergraduate and graduate programs across many colleges, with a strong emphasis on global studies and communication.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Research
  • Accreditation: NECHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $77,662/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 10:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 18,000 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 16,000 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 135 acres

Research Focus

Infectious disease research (NEIDL), engineering, communication and journalism, and global health.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Engineering, Questrom School of Business, Communication, Law, Medicine, Public Health, Education (Wheelock), Fine Arts, Hospitality Administration, Global Studies
