Bard College at Simon's Rock

Private liberal arts college in Great Barrington, Massachusetts, founded in 1966.

Bard College at Simon's Rock is an early-college institution admitting motivated students directly after the 10th or 11th grade into a rigorous liberal arts curriculum.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $78,000 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 8:1
  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 400 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 275 acres
  • Housing Capacity: 300 beds
  • Average Rent: $2,500 / month

Research Focus

Undergraduate research across sciences and humanities.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Arts, Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences)
