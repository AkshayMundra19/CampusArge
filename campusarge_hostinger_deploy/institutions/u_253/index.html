Morningside University

Location: Sioux City, United States

Morningside University combines a liberal arts foundation with professional programs, including a strong nursing program supported by a Mayo Clinic Health System partnership.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $50,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 12:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,200 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 200 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 68 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research across sciences and health professions.

Academic Programs

Business, Education, Nursing, Sciences, Liberal Arts
