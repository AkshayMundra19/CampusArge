California Institute of Technology (Caltech)

Location: Pasadena, United States

Caltech is a small, highly selective institute focused on science and engineering education and research, and manages NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL).

Research Focus

Astronomy and astrophysics (JPL, LIGO), chemistry, physics, planetary science, and computer and information science.

Academic Programs

Biology and Biological Engineering, Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Engineering and Applied Science, Geological and Planetary Sciences, Humanities and Social Sciences, Physics Mathematics and Astronomy
