Gardner-Webb University

Location: Boiling Springs, United States

Gardner-Webb University integrates Christian faith with liberal arts and professional programs, including nursing, education, and divinity.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: SACSCOC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $47,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 13:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,500 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 700 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 240 acres

Research Focus

Nursing and health science research, education and divinity scholarship.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Business, Divinity, Education, Nursing and Health Sciences
