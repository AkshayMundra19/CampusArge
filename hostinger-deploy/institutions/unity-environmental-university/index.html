Unity Environmental University

Location: New Gloucester, United States

Unity Environmental University specializes exclusively in environmental and sustainability education, offering hybrid and online formats alongside in-person study.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: NECHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $21,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 18:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 700 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 100 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 225 acres

Research Focus

Wildlife conservation, sustainable agriculture, and environmental policy research.

Academic Programs

Environmental Science, Sustainability, Wildlife and Fisheries, Marine Biology, Environmental Business and Policy, Captive Wildlife Care
