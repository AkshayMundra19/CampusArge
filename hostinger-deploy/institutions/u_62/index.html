Grinnell College

Private liberal arts college in Grinnell, Iowa, founded in 1846.

Grinnell is a highly selective liberal arts college known for its open curriculum, social justice tradition, and large endowment relative to its size.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $78,500 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Classification: Private Liberal Arts College
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 1,700 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 120 acres
  • Housing Capacity: 94 beds
  • Average Rent: $800 / month

Research Focus

Undergraduate research (Mentored Advanced Projects) across sciences and humanities.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social Studies), self-designed majors
