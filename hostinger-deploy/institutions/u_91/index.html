School of the Art Institute of Chicago (SAIC)

Private art and design college in Chicago, Illinois, founded in 1866, affiliated with the Art Institute of Chicago museum.

SAIC is a leading independent art and design school offering interdisciplinary studio-based education with direct access to the Art Institute of Chicago's collections.

Research Focus

Contemporary art practice, arts administration, and design research.

Academic Programs

Fine Arts, Art and Technology Studies, Fashion Design, Architecture Interior Architecture and Designed Objects, Art History, Arts Administration, Writing
