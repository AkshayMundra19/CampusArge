Delaware Valley University

Location: Doylestown, United States

Delaware Valley University is known for its agriculture, life sciences, and hands-on 'Experience 1896' learning model with working farms on campus.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $58,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 13:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,800 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 200 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 571 acres

Research Focus

Agricultural and life sciences research, applied undergraduate research.

Academic Programs

Agriculture, Business, Biology, Criminal Justice, Education
