University of La Verne

Location: La Verne, United States

University of La Verne offers a liberal arts foundation combined with professional programs in law, business, education, and health sciences, serving a diverse student body.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: WSCUC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $63,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 14:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,700 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 1,200 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 66 acres

Research Focus

Education and organizational leadership research, legal scholarship, and public administration.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Business and Public Management, Education and Organizational Leadership, Law, Health and Community Services
