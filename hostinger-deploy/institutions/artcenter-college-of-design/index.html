ArtCenter College of Design

Location: Pasadena, United States

ArtCenter College of Design is a leading professional art and design school known for its transportation design, industrial design, and entertainment design programs with close industry ties.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Design School
  • Accreditation: WSCUC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $70,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 8:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,000 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 300 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: Urban

Research Focus

Transportation and mobility design research, sustainable design, and applied design studios with industry partners.

Academic Programs

Transportation Design, Product Design, Graphic Design, Illustration, Film, Photography and Imaging, Environmental Design, Entertainment Design, Fine Art
