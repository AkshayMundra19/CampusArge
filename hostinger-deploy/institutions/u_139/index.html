The University of the Arts

Formerly a private arts university in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, founded in 1876; the university abruptly closed permanently in June 2024.

The University of the Arts was a specialized arts institution offering programs in visual arts, design, music, and performing arts before closing operations in 2024 due to financial difficulties; its website may retain limited historical/informational content.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $67,000 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 8:1
  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 1,000 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 18 acres
  • Housing Capacity: 400 beds
  • Average Rent: $1,800 / month

Research Focus

Historically focused on studio-based creative research in visual and performing arts (institution no longer operating).

Academic Programs

Historically: Art and Design, Music, Dance, Theatre, Media Arts (programs discontinued following the 2024 closure)
