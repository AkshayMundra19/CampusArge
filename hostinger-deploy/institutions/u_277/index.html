Belmont Abbey College

Private Catholic Benedictine liberal arts college in Belmont, North Carolina, founded in 1876.

Belmont Abbey College offers a Catholic liberal arts education grounded in the Benedictine monastic tradition.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $35,000 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 14:1
  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: SACSCOC Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 1,400 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 150 acres
  • Housing Capacity: 800 beds
  • Average Rent: $1,200 / month

Research Focus

Undergraduate research across sciences and humanities, theology scholarship.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts, Business, Education, Theology
