St. Ambrose University

Location: Davenport, United States

St. Ambrose University is a Catholic institution combining liberal arts with professional programs in business, education, and health sciences.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $50,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 12:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,500 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 800 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 60 acres

Research Focus

Health sciences research, business analytics, and education studies.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Business, Education, Health Sciences (Nursing, Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy)
