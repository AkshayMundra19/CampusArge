Dakota Wesleyan University

Location: Mitchell, United States

Dakota Wesleyan University combines a liberal arts core with professional programs, emphasizing leadership development through its McGovern Center.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $42,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 11:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 900 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 100 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 50 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research across sciences and business, leadership and public policy scholarship.

Academic Programs

Business, Education, Nursing, Liberal Arts, Leadership Studies
