Carleton College

Location: Northfield, United States

Carleton is a highly selective liberal arts college known for intellectual intensity, strong science programs, and a collaborative, non-competitive academic culture.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $78,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,105 undergraduates
  • Campus Acreage: 955 acres

Research Focus

Geology and environmental science, mathematics, and undergraduate research across disciplines.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Arts and Literature, Humanities, Natural Sciences and Mathematics, Social Sciences), Off-campus study programs
