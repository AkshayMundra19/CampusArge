College of the Ozarks

Location: Point Lookout, United States

College of the Ozarks requires all students to work on campus in lieu of paying tuition, combining a Christian liberal arts education with a strong work ethic and patriotic emphasis.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $19,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 14:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,500 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 0 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 1,000 acres

Research Focus

Agricultural and land stewardship research tied to its working campus enterprises.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences), Agriculture, Business, Education, Nursing
