Bellarmine University

Location: Louisville, United States

Bellarmine is a Catholic university offering liberal arts alongside strong nursing, business, and health science programs, with a growing Division I athletics profile.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Liberal Arts College
  • Accreditation: NWCCU Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $55,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,200 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 600 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 27 acres

Research Focus

Nursing and health sciences research, business analytics, and undergraduate STEM research.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Business, Nursing and Health Sciences, Education, Communication
