The College of Idaho

Location: Caldwell, United States

The College of Idaho is a liberal arts college known for its PEAK Curriculum, which requires students to pursue three areas of study for well-rounded preparation.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: NWCCU Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $45,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 10:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,000 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 50 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 50 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research across sciences and humanities.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences), Business, Education, Pre-Health
