Cogswell University of Silicon Valley

Location: San Jose, United States

Cogswell University focuses on career-focused programs in engineering, game design, and digital arts, with a co-op-style education model tied to Silicon Valley industry.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: WSCUC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $36,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 10:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 400 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 150 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 3 acres

Research Focus

Applied research in game design, engineering, and digital media.

Academic Programs

Game Design, Computer Science, Digital Art and Animation, Electrical Engineering, Business
