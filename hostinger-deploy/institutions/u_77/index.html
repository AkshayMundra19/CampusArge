The Juilliard School

Location: , United States

Juilliard is one of the world's leading conservatories, training students exclusively in dance, drama, and music at the highest professional level.

  • Cost of Attendance: $76,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 5:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 600 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 300 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: Urban Campus

Research Focus

Performance practice research, historical performance studies, and choreography.

Academic Programs

Dance, Drama, Music (Instrumental, Vocal Arts, Jazz, Historical Performance, Composition)
