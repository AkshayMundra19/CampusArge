New York University (NYU)

Location: , United States

NYU is one of the largest private research universities in the US, known for its urban campus integrated into New York City and a global network including campuses in Abu Dhabi and Shanghai.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Research
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $76,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 29,000 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 24,000 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: Urban Campus

Research Focus

Data science and AI (Courant, Center for Data Science), law, business (Stern), the arts (Tisch), and public service (Wagner).

Academic Programs

College of Arts and Science, Stern School of Business, Tandon School of Engineering, Law, Medicine (Grossman), Tisch School of the Arts, Steinhardt (Education/Culture), Wagner (Public Service), Gallatin, Journalism
