Baker University

Location: Baldwin City, United States

Baker University pairs a residential liberal arts college with professional and graduate schools serving traditional and working adult students.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $45,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 11:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,200 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 300 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 27 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research across sciences and business.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts, Business, Education, Nursing
