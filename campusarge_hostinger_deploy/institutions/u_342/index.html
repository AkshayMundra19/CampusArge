College of Saint Mary

Location: Omaha, United States

College of Saint Mary educates women at the undergraduate level within a Catholic Mercy tradition, offering coeducational graduate programs.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $36,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 10:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 700 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 150 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 80 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research in health sciences and education.

Academic Programs

Nursing, Business, Education, Health Sciences, Liberal Arts
