Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI)

Location: Troy, United States

RPI focuses on science, engineering, and technology education with an emphasis on innovation and entrepreneurship, and hosts one of the most powerful university-owned supercomputers.

Research Focus

Computational science (AI Research Center), biotechnology, nanotechnology, and cybersecurity.

Academic Programs

Engineering, Science, Architecture, Humanities Arts and Social Sciences, Lally School of Management, Information Technology and Web Science
