Princeton University

Private Ivy League research university in Princeton, New Jersey, founded in 1746, known for undergraduate teaching and research.

Princeton emphasizes undergraduate liberal arts education alongside graduate research, with no medical, law, or business school, and a strong tradition of independent senior thesis work.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $79,540 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 5:1
  • Classification: Private Ivy League Research University
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 5,400 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 600 acres
  • Housing Capacity: ~5,200 beds
  • Average Rent: $3,800 / month

Research Focus

Physics and astrophysics (Plasma Physics Lab), public policy (Woodrow Wilson School/SPIA), economics, computer science, molecular biology, and engineering.

Academic Programs

Engineering and Applied Science, Public and International Affairs (SPIA), Arts and Sciences (Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences), Architecture
