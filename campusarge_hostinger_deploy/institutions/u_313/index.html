Maryland University of Integrative Health

Location: Laurel, United States

Maryland University of Integrative Health is a graduate-only institution specializing in holistic and integrative health disciplines such as acupuncture, herbal medicine, and nutrition.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $30,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 10:1
  • Undergraduate Population: N/A
  • Graduate Population: 500 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 12 acres

Research Focus

Integrative and complementary health research.

Academic Programs

Acupuncture and Traditional Chinese Medicine, Herbal Medicine, Nutrition, Health and Wellness Coaching, Yoga Therapy
