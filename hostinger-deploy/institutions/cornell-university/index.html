Cornell University

Location: Ithaca, United States

Cornell is known for its 'any person, any study' philosophy, combining private Ivy League colleges with statutory (land-grant) colleges, and operating a New York City campus (Cornell Tech) and a medical college.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Ivy League Research University
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $77,952/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 15,000 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 7,000 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 4,800 acres

Research Focus

Agriculture and life sciences, veterinary medicine, engineering, hospitality management, computing and information science, and human ecology.

Academic Programs

Agriculture and Life Sciences, Arts and Sciences, Architecture Art and Planning, Business, Engineering, Human Ecology, Industrial and Labor Relations, Hotel Administration, Veterinary Medicine, Computing and Information Science, Cornell Tech, Medicine
