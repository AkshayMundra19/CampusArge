Harvard University

Location: Cambridge, United States

Harvard is a global leader in liberal arts education and research, comprising Harvard College and twelve graduate and professional schools. Its mission emphasizes educating citizens and citizen-leaders through transformative teaching and research.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Ivy League Research University
  • Accreditation: NECHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $76,763/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 7:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 6,700 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 14,500 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 5,076 acres

Research Focus

Medicine and public health, law, business and economics, government and policy, life sciences, engineering and applied sciences, humanities, and data science.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Business (HBS), Law, Medicine, Public Health, Education, Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS), Design, Divinity, Government (Kennedy School), Dental Medicine, Extension School
