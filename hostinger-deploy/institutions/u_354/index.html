Kansas Wesleyan University

Location: Salina, United States

Kansas Wesleyan University combines a liberal arts core with professional programs in a small residential campus community.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $46,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 11:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 700 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 100 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 28 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research across sciences and business.

Academic Programs

Business, Education, Nursing, Liberal Arts, Criminal Justice
