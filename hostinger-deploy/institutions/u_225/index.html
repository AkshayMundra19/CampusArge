John Brown University

Location: Siloam Springs, United States

John Brown University integrates evangelical Christian faith with a liberal arts core and career-focused programs in engineering, business, and construction management.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $44,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 14:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,200 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 400 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 200 acres

Research Focus

Engineering and construction management research, undergraduate business studies.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Business, Engineering, Construction Management, Education, Nursing, Bible and Theology
