University of Puget Sound

Location: Tacoma, United States

University of Puget Sound is a liberal arts institution known for its music school and strong study-abroad participation, in the Pacific Northwest.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Liberal Arts University
  • Accreditation: NWCCU Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $78,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 10:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,100 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 300 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 97 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research in the natural sciences, and music performance scholarship.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences), School of Music, Business Leadership Program
