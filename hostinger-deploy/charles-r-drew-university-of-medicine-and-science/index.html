Charles R. Drew University of Medicine and Science

Location: Los Angeles, United States

Charles R. Drew University trains physicians and health professionals with a mission of eliminating health disparities in underserved urban communities, partnering closely with UCLA.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: WSCUC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $35,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 7:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 300 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 700 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 11 acres

Research Focus

Health disparities and urban health research, biomedical and community health science research.

Academic Programs

Medicine (MD, in partnership with UCLA), Nursing, Physician Assistant Studies, Public Health, Health Sciences
