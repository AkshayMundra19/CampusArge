Concordia University Chicago

Location: River Forest, United States

Concordia University Chicago integrates a Lutheran faith foundation with liberal arts and professional programs, known for its teacher preparation programs.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $48,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 11:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,200 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 3,000 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 40 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research and education-focused scholarship, graduate research in curriculum and leadership.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Business, Education, Nursing, Theology, Graduate Studies
