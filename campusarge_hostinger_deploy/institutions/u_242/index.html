San Francisco Conservatory of Music

Location: San Francisco, United States

San Francisco Conservatory of Music offers intensive classical and contemporary music performance and composition training in a conservatory setting.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: WSCUC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $69,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 6:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 400 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 100 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 1 acres

Research Focus

Music performance and composition research, technology and applied music entrepreneurship (TMA program).

Academic Programs

Instrumental Performance, Composition, Conducting, Vocal Performance, Chamber Music, Technology and Applied Composition
