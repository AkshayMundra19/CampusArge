Harrisburg University of Science and Technology

Location: Harrisburg, United States

Harrisburg University is a STEM-focused institution without traditional campus housing, emphasizing applied technology education tied to industry needs.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $40,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 16:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 400 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 3,200 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: Urban

Research Focus

Applied research in artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and data analytics.

Academic Programs

Computer Science, Data Analytics, Biotechnology, Interactive Media, Project Leadership
