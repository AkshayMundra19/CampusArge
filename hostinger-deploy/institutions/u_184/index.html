Lake Forest College

Location: Lake Forest, United States

Lake Forest College is a liberal arts college near Chicago known for its Richter Scholar research program and internship connections to the city.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $69,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 12:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,600 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 100 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 107 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research (Richter Scholars program) across sciences and social sciences.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural Sciences and Mathematics, Social Sciences), Business, Education, Communication
