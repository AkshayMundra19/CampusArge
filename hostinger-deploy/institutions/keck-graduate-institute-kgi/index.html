Keck Graduate Institute (KGI)

Location: Claremont, United States

Keck Graduate Institute is a graduate-only institution focused on applied life sciences, biotechnology, and healthcare, bridging science education with industry practice.

Research Focus

Applied biomedical and biotechnology research, healthcare innovation, and pharmaceutical development.

Academic Programs

Applied Life Sciences, Biotechnology, Postgraduate Professional Studies, Pharmacy, Physician Assistant Studies
