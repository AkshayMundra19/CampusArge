Swarthmore College

Location: Swarthmore, United States

Swarthmore is a highly selective liberal arts college known for rigorous academics, an honors program, and a Quaker-founded tradition of social responsibility.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Liberal Arts
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $78,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 8:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,600 undergraduates
  • Campus Acreage: 425 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research across the natural sciences, engineering, and social sciences, with strong peace and conflict studies programs.

Academic Programs

Natural Sciences and Engineering, Social Sciences, Humanities, Engineering (one of few liberal arts colleges offering a full engineering major)
