Columbia University

Location: , United States

Columbia combines a core liberal arts curriculum with extensive graduate and professional education, leveraging its New York City location for global engagement in journalism, business, law, and international affairs.

  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $80,339/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 6:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 8,100 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 21,000 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 299 acres

Research Focus

Climate and earth science (Lamont-Doherty), journalism, international and public affairs (SIPA), biomedical research, data science, and nanoscience.

Academic Programs

Columbia College, Engineering and Applied Science (SEAS), Business, Law, Medicine (Vagelos), Journalism, International and Public Affairs (SIPA), Social Work, Arts, Nursing, General Studies
