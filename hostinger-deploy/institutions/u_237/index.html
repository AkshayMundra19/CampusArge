Alaska Pacific University

Private university in Anchorage, Alaska, founded in 1957.

Alaska Pacific University is a small university emphasizing outdoor studies, environmental science, and Alaska Native-focused education.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $37,000 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 8:1
  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: NWCCU Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 450 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 174 acres
  • Housing Capacity: 200 beds
  • Average Rent: $1,800 / month

Research Focus

Environmental and outdoor studies research, sustainability and Arctic-focused scholarship.

Academic Programs

Outdoor Studies, Environmental Science, Business, Liberal Studies, Alaska Native Studies
