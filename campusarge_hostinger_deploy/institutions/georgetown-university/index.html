International affairs and diplomacy (Walsh School), law and public policy, business ethics (McDonough), and global health.

Academic Programs

Georgetown College, Walsh School of Foreign Service, McDonough School of Business, Law, Medicine, Nursing and Health Studies, Public Policy (McCourt), Continuing Studies
