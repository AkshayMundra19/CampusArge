Smith College

Location: Northampton, United States

Smith is the largest women's college in the US, offering a liberal arts curriculum with strong programs in engineering (unique among women's colleges) and the sciences.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: NECHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $72,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,500 undergraduates
  • Campus Acreage: 147 acres

Research Focus

Engineering (Picker Engineering Program), neuroscience, and the study of women and gender.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts, Picker Engineering Program, Study of Women and Gender, Ada Comstock Scholars Program (for non-traditional students)
