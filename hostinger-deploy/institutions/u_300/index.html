Adler University

Location: Chicago, United States

Adler University is a graduate-only institution rooted in Alfred Adler's social-justice-oriented approach to psychology, training practitioners to address community and societal wellbeing.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $35,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 8:1
  • Undergraduate Population: N/A
  • Graduate Population: 1,300 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: N/A

Research Focus

Applied psychology and social justice research, community mental health scholarship.

Academic Programs

Clinical Psychology (PsyD), Counseling Psychology, Industrial-Organizational Psychology, Public Policy, Social Work
