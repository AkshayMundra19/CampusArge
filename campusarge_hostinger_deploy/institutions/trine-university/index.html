Trine University

Location: Angola, United States

Trine University emphasizes career-focused education with strong engineering and business programs, alongside a liberal arts core.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $50,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 15:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,200 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 500 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 450 acres

Research Focus

Applied engineering research, motorsports engineering, and business analytics.

Academic Programs

Allen School of Engineering and Computing, Jannen School of Business, Arts and Sciences, Health Sciences, Design
