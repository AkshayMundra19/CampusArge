University of Saint Joseph

Location: West Hartford, United States

University of Saint Joseph offers a Catholic liberal arts foundation alongside professional programs, including a notable pharmacy school and health sciences programs.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: NECHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $57,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,000 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 500 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 90 acres

Research Focus

Pharmaceutical sciences research, health science and education scholarship.

Academic Programs

Pharmacy, Nursing, Business, Education, Health Sciences, Liberal Arts
