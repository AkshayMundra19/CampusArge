Grove City College

Location: Grove City, United States

Grove City College is known for its conservative Christian values, refusal of federal funding to preserve independence, and strong engineering and business programs.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $32,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 13:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,300 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: N/A
  • Campus Acreage: 180 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research in engineering and the natural sciences, economics scholarship (free-market emphasis).

Academic Programs

Engineering, Entrepreneurship Business and Management, Humanities, Sciences, Education
