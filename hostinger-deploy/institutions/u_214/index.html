Marywood University

Location: Scranton, United States

Marywood University is a Catholic institution known for its architecture, social work, and art therapy programs alongside a broad liberal arts curriculum.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $52,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 12:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,900 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 1,200 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 115 acres

Research Focus

Social work and art therapy research, architecture and design studies.

Academic Programs

Architecture, Art and Design, Business, Education, Social Work, Health and Human Services, Music
