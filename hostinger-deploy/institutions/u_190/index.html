Iona University

Location: New Rochelle, United States

Iona University is a Catholic institution offering liberal arts and business education with a strong Division I athletics program and proximity to New York City.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $58,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 14:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,900 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 900 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 45 acres

Research Focus

Business analytics, health science research, and criminal justice studies.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences, Business (LaPenta School), Education, Health Sciences
