Stanford University

Location: Stanford, United States

Stanford's mission is to educate future leaders and promote interdisciplinary teaching and research across the humanities, sciences, engineering, and professional fields, with a strong culture of entrepreneurship.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Research University
  • Accreditation: WASC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $78,218/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 5:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 7,000 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 9,400 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 8,180 acres

Research Focus

Artificial intelligence (HAI), computer science, bioengineering, medicine, energy and sustainability (Doerr School), business innovation, and law and policy.

Academic Programs

Engineering, Humanities and Sciences, Business (GSB), Law, Medicine, Education, Earth Sustainability and Energy (Doerr School)
