Case Western Reserve University

Location: Cleveland, United States

Case Western Reserve is a research university with strong programs in engineering, medicine, law, and management, closely linked to Cleveland's medical and cultural institutions.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Research
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $80,080/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 5,792 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 6,300 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 267 acres

Research Focus

Biomedical engineering, macromolecular science, medicine (Cleveland Clinic partnership), and management science.

Academic Programs

College of Arts and Sciences, Case School of Engineering, Weatherhead School of Management, Law, Medicine, Dental Medicine, Nursing (Bolton), Social Work (Mandel)
