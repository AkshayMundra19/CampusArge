Milwaukee School of Engineering (MSOE)

Location: Milwaukee, United States

MSOE is a small, career-focused university specializing in engineering, computer science, business, and nursing, with strong industry partnerships and hands-on lab instruction.

Research Focus

Applied engineering research, robotics and automation, and biomedical engineering.

Academic Programs

Engineering (Mechanical, Electrical, Biomedical, Civil, Architectural), Computer Science and Software Engineering, Business, Nursing
