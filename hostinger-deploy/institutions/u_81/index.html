Hobart and William Smith Colleges

Private coordinate liberal arts colleges in Geneva, New York, founded in 1822 (Hobart) and 1908 (William Smith).

Hobart and William Smith are coordinate colleges for men and women sharing a single faculty and campus, offering a shared liberal arts curriculum with separate deans and athletics.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $78,000 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 10:1
  • Classification: Private Liberal Arts Colleges
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 2,200 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 320 acres
  • Housing Capacity: 1,800 beds
  • Average Rent: $900 / month

Research Focus

Undergraduate research in environmental studies (Finger Lakes Institute) and the sciences.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences), Environmental Studies (Finger Lakes Institute)
