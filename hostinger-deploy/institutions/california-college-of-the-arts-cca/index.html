California College of the Arts (CCA)

Location: San Francisco, United States

CCA is an independent art and design college offering studio-based undergraduate and graduate programs in fine arts, design, architecture, and writing.

Research Focus

Design strategy research, sustainable architecture, and interdisciplinary studio practice.

Academic Programs

Architecture, Fine Arts, Graphic Design, Industrial Design, Fashion Design, Illustration, Animation, Writing and Literature, Curatorial Practice
