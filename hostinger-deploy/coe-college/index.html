Coe College

Location: Cedar Rapids, United States

Coe College is a liberal arts college known for its strong pre-professional preparation and a broad range of majors within a small residential community.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: HLC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $64,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 11:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 1,400 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: N/A
  • Campus Acreage: 70 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research across sciences and business, creative writing scholarship.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural Sciences and Mathematics, Social Sciences), Business Administration, Education, Music
