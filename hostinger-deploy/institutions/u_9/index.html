Johns Hopkins University

Private research university in Baltimore, Maryland, founded in 1876, recognized as the first US research university.

Johns Hopkins pioneered the American research university model, integrating graduate research with undergraduate education, and is a global leader in biomedical research and public health.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $79,340 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 7:1
  • Classification: Private Research University
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 6,000 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 140 acres
  • Housing Capacity: ~4,500 beds
  • Average Rent: $1,400 / month

Research Focus

Biomedical research and medicine, public health (Bloomberg School), international relations (SAIS), applied physics (APL), and engineering.

Academic Programs

Arts and Sciences (Krieger), Engineering (Whiting), Medicine, Nursing, Public Health (Bloomberg), Business (Carey), Education, Advanced International Studies (SAIS), Peabody Institute (Music)
