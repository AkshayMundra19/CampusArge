California Institute of the Arts (CalArts)

Location: Valencia, United States

CalArts is a leading conservatory-style arts institution known for its programs in animation, film, theater, dance, and music, with strong ties to the entertainment industry.

Research Focus

Experimental animation, digital arts, and interdisciplinary creative practice.

Academic Programs

Art, Critical Studies, Dance, Film/Video, Music, Theater, Character Animation, Experimental Animation
