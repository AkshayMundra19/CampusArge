Muhlenberg College

Location: Allentown, United States

Muhlenberg is a selective liberal arts college known for strong theatre and pre-health programs and a Lutheran heritage of academic excellence.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $74,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 10:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 2,000 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 300 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 75 acres

Research Focus

Undergraduate research in the natural sciences and theatre/performance studies.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Humanities, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences), Theatre and Dance, Public Health
