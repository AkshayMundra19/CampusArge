Yale University

Location: New Haven, United States

Yale is organized around Yale College's liberal arts curriculum and a wide array of graduate and professional schools, with a strong emphasis on the arts, humanities, and residential college life.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Ivy League Research University
  • Accreditation: NECHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $90,550/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 6:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 4,700 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 7,500 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 1,015 acres

Research Focus

Humanities and law, medicine and public health, environmental studies, art and architecture, and molecular and cellular biology.

Academic Programs

Yale College, Law, Medicine, Management (SOM), Art, Architecture, Drama, Divinity, Forestry and Environmental Studies, Music, Nursing, Public Health, Engineering and Applied Science
