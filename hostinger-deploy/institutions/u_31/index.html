Boston College

Private Jesuit Catholic research university in Chestnut Hill, Massachusetts, founded in 1863.

Boston College blends Jesuit Catholic educational tradition with liberal arts and professional education, emphasizing formation of the whole person alongside academic rigor.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $77,308 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 11:1
  • Classification: Private Research
  • Accreditation: NECHE Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 9,377 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 338 acres
  • Housing Capacity: ~7,600 beds
  • Average Rent: $3,000 / month

Research Focus

Theology and Catholic studies, social work (Boston College School of Social Work), and finance.

Academic Programs

Morrissey College of Arts and Sciences, Carroll School of Management, Lynch School of Education and Human Development, Connell School of Nursing, Law, Social Work, Theology and Ministry
