Otis College of Art and Design

Private art and design college in Los Angeles, California, founded in 1918.

Otis College of Art and Design is an independent art and design college known for its fashion design, animation, and product design programs tied to LA's creative industries.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $68,000 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 7:1
  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: WSCUC Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 1,200 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 5 acres
  • Housing Capacity: 400 beds
  • Average Rent: $3,200 / month

Research Focus

Design innovation and creative economy research, sustainable fashion design.

Academic Programs

Fashion Design, Product Design, Animation, Graphic Design, Fine Arts, Photography, Architecture/Landscape/Interiors, Toy Design
