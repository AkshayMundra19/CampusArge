Logan University

Private health sciences university in Chesterfield, Missouri, founded in 1935.

Logan University is a chiropractic-focused health sciences institution offering doctoral and graduate degrees in health and wellness fields.

  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 8:1
  • Classification: Private

  • Undergraduate Students: 100 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 112 acres
  • Housing Capacity: N/A

Research Focus

Chiropractic and health sciences research.
