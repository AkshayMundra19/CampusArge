Claremont McKenna College

Private liberal arts college in Claremont, California, founded in 1946, part of the Claremont Colleges.

Claremont McKenna is a selective liberal arts college with a focus on economics, government, and public affairs, preparing students for leadership in business, government, and public policy.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Cost of Attendance: $85,000 / year
  • Student-to-Faculty Ratio: 8:1
  • Classification: Private Liberal Arts College
  • Accreditation: WSCUC Accredited
  • Undergraduate Students: 1,400 undergraduates
  • Campus Size: 69 acres
  • Housing Capacity: 1,200 beds
  • Average Rent: $2,000 / month

Research Focus

Public policy (Rose Institute), economics and finance (Financial Economics Institute), and government studies.

Academic Programs

Liberal Arts (Economics, Government, Philosophy Politics and Economics, Natural Sciences, Humanities), Robert Day School of Economics and Finance
