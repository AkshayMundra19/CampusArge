Rice University

Location: Houston, United States

Rice combines a small liberal arts college atmosphere with major research programs, emphasizing undergraduate teaching, residential colleges, and close ties to Houston's energy and medical sectors.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private Research
  • Accreditation: SACSCOC Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $69,557/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 6:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 4,000 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 3,000 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 300 acres

Research Focus

Nanotechnology and materials science, space science (historic NASA ties), bioengineering, and computational science.

Academic Programs

Humanities, Social Sciences, Natural Sciences, Engineering, Architecture, Music (Shepherd School), Business (Jones Graduate School)
