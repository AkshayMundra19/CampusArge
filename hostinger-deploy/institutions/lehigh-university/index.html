Lehigh University

Location: Bethlehem, United States

Lehigh is known for integrating engineering, business, and the liberal arts, with strong industry partnerships and a compact, research-active campus.

Key Institutional Facts

  • Classification: Private
  • Accreditation: MSCHE Accredited
  • Cost of Attendance: $70,000/year
  • Student-Faculty Ratio: 9:1
  • Undergraduate Population: 5,200 undergraduates
  • Graduate Population: 1,800 graduate students
  • Campus Acreage: 2,350 acres

Research Focus

Materials science, nanotechnology, and supply chain/business analytics.

Academic Programs

College of Arts and Sciences, P.C. Rossin College of Engineering and Applied Science, College of Business, College of Education, College of Health
